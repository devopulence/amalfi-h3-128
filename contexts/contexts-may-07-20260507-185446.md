### Architectural notes

1. **POC-4 by-pointer convention is the law.** When the user's prompt assumed the shim covered all 16 public functions and it didn't, the right move was to extend the shim (Option A) rather than reach around it with direct `__uint128_t` calls from cffi. POC-4 specifically warned about the latter (`__uint128_t` lacks stable register-passing ABI on Windows MSVC; fragile elsewhere). The shim extension was 60 lines of additive C and ctest stayed green.

2. **Hierarchy boundary epsilon at (40.33, -73.99) is a real geometric phenomenon, not a bug.** First seen in Session 7-py tests (`test_res16_child_has_res15_parent`). Reproduced in Session 8 at scale: 7.22% of random Monmouth-bbox coords land within ε of a res-19 cell edge, where `cell_to_parent(c20, 19) ≠ latlng_to_cell(lat, lng, 19)`. The hierarchy contract (child contained by parent) holds — what differs is which of the equally-close coarser cells the projection rounding picks. Both Python tests and validate_local.py adopted "anchor at parent center" for tests where strict equality is asserted, and "report as BOUNDARY informational" for diagnostic surfaces.

3. **Single-platform round-trip is bit-deterministic on macOS arm64 + Apple libm.** Confirmed in Session 8: 0.000000 mm round-trip at every one of 103 real images and at every res 19/20 idempotent re-index check. Cross-platform variability (the actual purpose of `latlng_within_tolerance` in test.h) is structural — same g_in may produce different h1 on glibc vs Apple libm. Linux x86_64 cross-validation is the next legitimate test (Session 9 closes naturally via Databricks executor).

4. **CI fix landed cleanly via destination-session pickup.** The user's recommendation to "have the destination session fix it" worked — that session naturally honored the fork's pre-commit ctest discipline and ran `gh run watch` to confirm green before declaring done. Closed the loop in one place.

Mirrors the existing h3-128-audit pattern. `.gitignore` allowlist updated to track `.claude/skills/amalfi-h3-128/**` (rest of `.claude/` stays ephemeral).
